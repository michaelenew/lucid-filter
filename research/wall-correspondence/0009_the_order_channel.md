**The distinction the S³ toy could not show**: our curved-tier
filter (the sibling's S³ filter) handles nonabelian *state* on a
single chain — and one chain has one order. The new channel appears
only when several innovations are *composed into a summary*:
abelian innovations compose by sum (order-free); nonabelian ones by
ordered product, and with three or more factors the order changes
the summary. Because the summary's class is conjugation-invariant,
cyclic orders collapse: of 3! arrangements, exactly the **parity**
survives, carried by the commutator.

**The experiment**: three labeled S³ innovations composed in a
random order; the composite read through matched apparatus noise;
an order-aware bank vs the honest order-blind mixture, both knowing
the innovations. The prequential gap *is* the channel:

| τ | gap (nats/triple) | hard-decision bound |
|---|---|---|
| 0.05 | 0.0169 | 0.0061 |
| 0.10 | 0.0306 | 0.0109 |
| 0.20 | 0.0547 | 0.0202 |
| 0.40 | 0.0887 | 0.0365 |

U(1) control: summaries identical in every order — the gap is
exactly zero. No abelian filter (Kalman, circle) can ever see this
channel; that is a theorem, not a failure to look.

**What this means here**: (1) any bank whose innovations carry
group structure and get composed into block summaries must treat
arrival order as informative — order-blindness has a computable
prequential price; (2) the converse is an instrument, sibling to
the 0006 detector: measurable order-information in composed
summaries certifies nonabelian structure in the stream's source;
(3) on the physics side this is their causal layer — time's order
carries information exactly where the theory is nonabelian — so the
filter's inherent causality is not a mismatch with their Euclidean
measure but the feature that becomes load-bearing at this tier.
