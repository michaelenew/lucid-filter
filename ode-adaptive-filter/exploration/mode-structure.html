Which mode was excited

Every disturbance splits over the roots, weighted by the amplitude each mode contributes to the observation. The roots are the real channels; derivative names are a coordinate choice laid over them.

Forcing noise is a mixture, not a corner. That is why it never separates from a pure top-derivative kick, and why naming the corners by derivative rather than by root eventually breaks.
