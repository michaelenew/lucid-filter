## What happened over there

Their item 6 (classical tests) **failed**, and not numerically. With
matter integrated out, their induced action for a symmetric background
metric is

    Γ⁽²⁾[A] = tr(BA²) − tr(BABA),  B = D(DᵀD)⁺Dᵀ a projector

and because B is a projector this is identically

> **Γ⁽²⁾[A] = ‖(1−B)AB‖²_F ≥ 0**

for *every* symmetric A, including the conformal mode. Linearised
Einstein-Hilbert requires the conformal mode to carry the **opposite**
sign to the transverse-traceless modes. So their induced action cannot
be Einstein-Hilbert, γ came out ≈ −1 rather than +1 across two source
conventions and two volumes, and the theory predicts **zero light
bending**.

A sum of PSD forms is PSD, so no field content fixes it.

## The tension this creates on our side

[`0010`](0010_gravity_in_the_filter.md) measured **lensing** here —
influence routes around a trust well, 2× faster than through — and
[`0011`](0011_the_trust_field.md) measured Newton's 1/r as the Green
function with α = 1.02. [`0012`](0012_the_force_law.md) measured
attraction as a code gradient, C(r) = a − b/r^1.04.

**Those are not in doubt as measurements. What is now in doubt is
whether they are the same phenomenon as GR's.**

A 1/r Green function is *not* evidence of a spin-2 theory — a scalar
theory has one too, and their item 4 measured one (ratio 1.0722,
spread 1.68% over a factor 15 in r) in the very action that then
failed γ. Newton's law is the part scalar gravity gets **right**; it
is bending that separates the two, and 1919 is exactly where scalar
gravity died.

So the sharp question for this side is:

> **Is our measured lensing bending of null rays, or is it routing of
> a signal through a medium with a varying refractive index?**

Those look alike in a filter and are *not* alike in relativity. A
conformally flat metric leaves null geodesics untouched but absolutely
does change propagation delay — 0010's Shapiro measurement (+483
steps) is a *delay*, which Nordström also has. Delay does not
discriminate. **Only deflection of a null ray does.**
