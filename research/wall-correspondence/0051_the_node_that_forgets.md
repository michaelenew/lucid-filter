## The distinction 0166 missed

Balanced representations (j⁺ = j⁻) are Barrett–Crane's **simplicity
condition**. Intertwiner-independence is Barrett–Crane's **defect**.
They are different properties, and 0166 treated having the first as
evidence of having the second.

## An intertwiner, without the group theory

At a node, n channels meet. Each carries a magnitude. The node is
**closed** — the channel vectors sum to zero — and a global rotation
is unobservable. What is estimable is the **shape** of the closed
configuration modulo rotation. The question is whether that shape
carries information the magnitudes do not.

Measured as the rank of the constraint Jacobian:

| n | measured dim | 2n − 6 |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | **0** | 0 |
| 4 | **2** | 2 |
| 5 | 4 | 4 |
| 6 | 6 | 6 |

> **At n = 3 the shape space is a point.** Three closed vectors of
> fixed length have no freedom once the rotation is quotiented — and
> `dim Inv(j₁,j₂,j₃) ≤ 1` correspondingly. A 3-valent node has nothing
> beyond its magnitudes.
>
> **At n = 4 it is two-dimensional.** A tetrahedron has four faces, so
> its node is 4-valent: **its face areas do not determine its shape.**
> That residual shape is the intertwiner.

## What blindness would cost

A magnitude-only summary collapses every shape sharing the same
magnitudes into one symbol:

| four equal spins j | dim Inv | nats discarded per node |
|---|---|---|
| 1/2 | 2 | 0.6931 |
| 3/2 | 4 | 1.3863 |
| **5/2** | **6** | **1.7918** |

At their band limit (M = 6, j ≤ 5/2) the worst case is **ln 6 = 1.79
nats per node**, and it is never zero above j = 1/2.

## What that does to a capacity argument

Their derivation of κ runs on capacity: with M usable symbols,
capacity is maximised when the symbols are equiprobable, forcing flat
multiplicities. **That argument counts states.** Run it honestly at a
node and the states are the label assignment *and* the shape.

> **The capacity argument does not force the Barrett–Crane form. It
> forbids it.** A principle that maximises capacity cannot select an
> amplitude that discards 1.79 nats per node.

And it does not touch κ, which is fixed by the multiplicity profile
over **face** labels — the distribution capacity equalises. The
intertwiner is a separate degree of freedom at the **node**.

**Falsifiable prediction issued to the port:** intertwiner structure
should leave κ = 16.0001 exactly unchanged, because the curvature at
the identity is a property of the face weight alone. If κ moves, this
argument is wrong and the sectors are not separable.

## What the port found

Better than predicted. Their 0158 asked the prior question — is the
weight *actually* intertwiner-blind — and the answer is **no**. In a
lattice gauge theory the Haar integral over each link is itself a
projector onto the invariants, and its **rank equals dim Inv, not 1**.
The intertwiner space is generated by the measure; no face weight can
remove it.

So the prediction was never tested, because the premise was false.
The program has simplicity at the face level and full-rank
intertwiners at the node — the first Barrett–Crane property without
the second.
